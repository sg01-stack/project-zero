How to interpret the detector

A short, isolated attack followed by a rapid decay is a transient candidate. Whisper-like sound must persist and vary over time. Brief consonants and pauses do not immediately change a speech turn's style.

Whisper, breath, wind, and background noise can overlap acoustically. Whisper-like is tentative; uncertain audio is reported separately. For dependable word detection, connect a local speech model and pass its transcript with the current turn ID.

Analysis uses 50 ms audio frames. Turns finish after 800 ms of silence, or split at 30 seconds. Headphones help prevent narrative playback from entering the microphone.

Audio analysis stays on this device. When Evolus is configured on the local backend, completed turn JSON is sent to its remote agent; microphone PCM is not sent. No browser speech recognition is enabled. See the README for configuration and integration.
