# What “two Evolus building blocks” means

The participant supplied the kickoff transcript for AMD Developer Hackathon Act 3. The speaker lists **agents, workflows, document extractors, AI tools, and knowledge libraries**, then says to use at least two. That rule is for the separate **Evolus Business Agents on AMD** challenge.

It does **not** mean two apps, two installations of Studio, two agents automatically counting as different feature types, or two model deployments. An AMD model plus an Evolus agent is not evidence of two Evolus platform building blocks. The browser's Whisper and Kokoro models are not Evolus building blocks either.

## EchoSphere's selected submission

EchoSphere is prepared for **Track 4: Create a New Kind of Experience**. The supplied transcript explicitly includes interactive stories, adaptive sound/visual environments, and accessible media in that track. Its special demo requirement is to show differences across at least two audiences, situations, or inputs. The app's quiet/distant comparison and the presentation guide address this.

The current integration uses **one Evolus agent**, via the OpenAI-compatible API, with the participant's AMD-hosted model. We do not claim eligibility for the business-agent prize or claim a second platform feature has been connected.

## If entering the Evolus business-agent challenge as well

That needs additional work and a clear real business process, not a renamed entertainment demo. A relevant example could be an experience studio taking an approved story brief through knowledge-grounded scene generation and human review.

A genuine second component could be an Evolus **knowledge library** containing approved story canon, linked to the agent and demonstrably retrieved during a response. Another could be a real **workflow** that routes a generated experience for review. Merely adding a document to GitHub or writing a workflow diagram does not connect that platform feature.

Before claiming that prize's requirements, configure and test the component in the actual Evolus workspace, preserve evidence of its use, and demonstrate the complete business process and human-review point. Keep the open model on AMD, verify tool calling if the component uses tools, use the app API, provide the public repository and run instructions, and never commit credentials.
